Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 10/22/2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Frostbridge AI, Inc, doing business as Frostbridge (“Frostbridge,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with our websites, applications, SaaS platform, agents, browser extensions, integrations, support, sales, and related services.
This Privacy Policy is written for website visitors, prospects, account users, administrators, and individuals who interact directly with Frostbridge. Customer Data processed by Frostbridge on behalf of a business customer is primarily governed by the Frostbridge Customer Agreement and Data Processing Addendum.
1. Information we collect
We may collect the following categories of information:
* Account and contact information: name, business email, company name, job title, phone number, login credentials, organization identifiers, and user profile details.
* Billing and commercial information: subscription plan, seats, billing contact, invoices, payment status, tax information, and payment metadata. Payment card details may be processed by our payment provider and not stored by Frostbridge.
* Product and usage information: login events, admin actions, seat changes, feature usage, API usage, audit logs, product telemetry, error logs, and support interactions.
* Security and integration data: data made available through customer-connected systems such as identity providers, cloud accounts, code repositories, SaaS tools, AI tools, browsers, endpoints, agents, extensions, and other integrations. This may include security events, device metadata, browser/app activity, prompt and response metadata or content, network or process metadata, access events, repository metadata, and related context depending on customer configuration.
* Website and device information: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, operating system, referral URL, pages viewed, and cookie or similar technology data.
* Communications: emails, chat messages, demo requests, support tickets, call notes, and feedback.
2. Sources of information
We collect information directly from users and administrators, from customer-connected systems, from agents/extensions deployed by customers, from service providers, from authentication and payment providers, from public sources, and automatically through cookies, logs, and similar technologies.
3. How we use information
We use information to:
* provide, operate, secure, monitor, and improve Frostbridge;
* create and administer accounts, subscriptions, seats, billing, and support;
* connect and operate integrations, agents, APIs, and security workflows;
* generate alerts, recommendations, risk scores, reports, and AI-assisted outputs;
* detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to security, fraud, abuse, reliability, and legal issues;
* communicate about products, security notices, updates, and administrative matters;
* conduct analytics, debugging, research, product development, and de-identified or aggregated analysis;
* comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect rights.
4. Customer-controlled data
Business customers decide which systems to connect, which agents/extensions to deploy, which users to monitor, and what data to make available to Frostbridge. Customers are responsible for providing required notices, obtaining required consents, and ensuring that their use of Frostbridge complies with employment, privacy, monitoring, BYOD, works council, sector-specific, and other applicable laws.
5. AI processing
Frostbridge may use AI models and automated systems to classify, summarize, enrich, score, prioritize, or recommend actions based on data processed through the Services. AI outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, or require human review. Unless otherwise stated in the Customer Agreement or DPA, Frostbridge uses Customer Data to provide and improve the Services, including security, reliability, debugging, detection quality, and product functionality. Customer Data is not sold.
6. How we disclose information
We may disclose information to:
* service providers and subprocessors that host, secure, operate, support, analyze, or improve Frostbridge;
* payment processors, authentication providers, cloud providers, database providers, email providers, support tools, analytics tools (including Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, as described in Section 7), and AI infrastructure providers;
* customer administrators and authorized users within the customer’s organization;
* professional advisors, auditors, insurers, and legal counsel;
* government authorities or third parties when required by law or to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of Frostbridge;
* parties involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, corporate reorganization, or sale of assets.
7. Cookies and similar technologies
We may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies for essential site functionality, authentication, analytics, security, preferences, and marketing.
* Essential: cookies and local storage needed to sign in, keep a session secure, and remember settings. The Services do not work without them.
* Product analytics: the Frostbridge dashboard, including customer-branded portals that Frostbridge hosts, uses Google Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how the product is used, for example which pages are visited and which setup steps are completed. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (such as _ga and _ga_<ID>) and receives pseudonymous identifiers that Frostbridge assigns (an internal user identifier, organization identifier, role, and plan), page paths with tokens and query strings removed, the site hostname, and browser and device information. Frostbridge does not send names, email addresses, organization names, credentials, or the content of prompts, files, or sessions to Google Analytics. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled, and Frostbridge does not use Google Analytics for advertising. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
* Usage statistics: Frostbridge also computes aggregate statistics about use of the Services, such as daily totals of events processed and active accounts, as described in Section 3. These statistics do not identify individuals.
